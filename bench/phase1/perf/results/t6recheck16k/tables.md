## Host load at each block start

The driver waits before every block for the 1-minute load to fall below 1 and the 5-minute load below 2 (on a run of the older driver only before the first block). A block is flagged `NOT QUIET` when the 5-minute load at its start was above 2 (the harness rule). The 5-minute load after a block, and at every repetition start after the first, is high by itself: the daemon's blocked writer threads and the fio jobs of the measurement count in the load average (hundreds), so only the block-start figure says whether the host was quiet before the block.

## Headline: per arm, rep 1 apart from reps 2-5

Regular pass wall = `wall_ms` of the non-Recheck, non-urgent pass lines that ran compaction. Compaction us/unit = compact-phase ms over compact-phase units of the pass lines in the window. GC share = collector time over the repetition's wall time, passes clipped to the window, urgent passes counted by their wall time; mean over the reps of the group. Onset = the engine's `write throttle on` line (coalesced to 5 s; the collector logs no pressure change of its own); `fresh` = the line's headroom was still >= 0.9 x soft.
