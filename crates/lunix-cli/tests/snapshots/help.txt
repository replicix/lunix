Command line client of lunixd

Usage: lunix [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  volume  Create and list volumes
  attach  Attach a volume and expose it as /dev/lunix/NAME
  detach  Detach a volume
  status  Show one volume's status, or every volume's
  resize  Grow a volume
  space   Show or change a volume's space policy
  gc      Run a garbage-collection pass, a dry run, or show the collector state
  fsck    Check (and repair) a detached volume offline
  key     List and change the key slots of a detached volume

Options:
      --socket <PATH>  The control socket of lunixd [default: /run/lunix/ctl.sock]
      --timeout <T>    How long to wait for the daemon's reply (500ms, 60s, 5m, 1h); attach and gc can be slow, so the default is generous [default: 10m]
  -h, --help           Print help
  -V, --version        Print version

Exit codes:
  0  success
  1  generic error (including a daemon that cannot be reached)
  2  usage error
  3  busy (the volume or device is in use)
  4  not found
  5  no space
  6  downgrade, wrong key, rollback or fork (the credential or history does not match)
  7  journal damage found and the attach asked to refuse

Sizes are digits with an optional K, M, G or T suffix (powers of 1024).
Secrets are read from a terminal prompt, --passphrase-fd or a key file, never from
the command line or the environment.
