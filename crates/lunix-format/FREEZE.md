# `lunix-format` freeze checklist

Draft written by M1.A0 (2026-11-12), revised after the independent A0
review. The layouts are specified in
[`docs/reference/on-disk-formats.md`](../../docs/reference/on-disk-formats.md)
and the attach results in
[`docs/reference/attach-matrix.md`](../../docs/reference/attach-matrix.md).
M1.A ticks an item when its type, encoder, decoder and golden files exist
and pass (plan M1.A steps 4 and 5). Only the lead fills in the last line,
after the maintainer's review. After that line is filled, changing any
golden file is a format change and needs the maintainer.

Golden files live in `crates/lunix-format/tests/golden/`, each as `<name>.bin`
plus a `<name>.hex` rendering, generated by the documented command and
compared byte for byte. Files named `a0-*` reproduce a worked example of
on-disk-formats.md §23 byte for byte. `kat-vectors.json` is copied to
`crates/lunix-format/tests/vectors/`.

**Amendment 2026-11-14** (maintainer decision
of 2026-11-14 after the M12a re-review, finding R1; on-disk-formats.md §5.2
and §5.3). Item 2: bytes 56-63 of the journal record header, reserved and
zero in the signed layout, become `flushed` (u64): the highest `write_seq`
that a completed group flush had made durable on every lane when the record
was appended, always below the record's `write_seq`. A record written under
the signed layout (zeros there) reads as watermark 0, so the existing item 2
goldens (`a0-journal-batch-two-records.bin`, `02-journal-record-*.bin`,
`02-journal-torn-batch.bin`, `02-journal-resync.bin`) are unchanged byte for
byte; the reader no longer requires the bytes to be zero and rejects
`flushed >= write_seq`. DELTA regions and the memfd sink write 0. Recovery
reports the highest watermark found; a gap or damage at or below it is
real loss (what `on_journal_damage = refuse` refuses), everything above it
is crash residue (ADR-4).

Re-signed by: the maintainer (Attila Nagy) on 2026-11-14 (amendment: journal flushed watermark; merge gated on an independent review and the four-queue kill -9 + refuse cell)
