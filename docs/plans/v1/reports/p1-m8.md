### Semantics fixed here

- **Limit.** `fs_ceiling = used_at_reading + fs_free - floor`; `reserve = 2 * segment_size + settings.headroom`;
  `limit = min(cache_max, fs_ceiling - reserve)`. The room is counted from the ledger level the reading belongs to, so
  the ledger's own bytes are not counted as free space and not charged twice. `refresh` samples the ledger before and
  after `statvfs`, retries 3 times if it moved, then uses the lower sample. `headroom_total` in the snapshot is `reserve`.
- **Refresh**: every 1 s on the gate's clock (background thread, `background_timers`), on demand inside `reserve` when
  `used + bytes` reaches 95 % of the limit (synchronous `statvfs`, no lock held), and `refresh()`. `EIO` from the probe
  makes the volume read-only.
- **Waiters**: FIFO, charged at grant, expired by `tick` (also by the thread, sleeping until the nearest deadline);
  an expired or cancelled head never blocks the ones behind it. `fatal_io` expires all parked requests.
- **Headroom**: `reserve_headroom` fails with `NoSpace` only if `used + Headroom bounds + request > limit + reserve`; the
  `Headroom` pseudo-kind is additionally capped at `reserve`, which guarantees `used + bounds <= limit + reserve` (the
  gate invariant "used exceeds the limit by at most the headroom"). `reserve(Headroom, ..)` is `Invalid`.
- **Ladder**: thread runs registered steps in kind order until the head waiter fits; started by a parked request, by a
  refused one in `error` mode (for the next write), and once a second while requests stay parked. Errors skip the step.
- `reserve` with `bytes == 0` is `Reserved` at once.

## Deviations and open choices

1. **One mutex instead of atomic counters.** The plan says atomic counters; the iface allows "short locks". A mutex makes
   `transfer` and `snapshot` atomic for observers by construction. Critical sections are a few additions.
2. **No `loom`**; the 60 s stress run is used (the plan allows either). `loom` is not a pinned dependency.
3. **`Headroom` pseudo-kind capped at the reserve** (stricter than `MemSpace`). Transfers *into* `Headroom` from another
   kind are not capped (not in the protocol); the model skips them.
4. **`cache_max` caps the write limit only**; the reserve is carved from the file system's room, never from `cache_max`.
5. **`reconfigure`** takes effect for new requests and re-evaluates the queue; parked requests keep their deadline and
   are not failed when `on_full` becomes `error`. In `error` mode, requests ignore leftover waiters.
6. **Registered steps that hold the gate form a cycle**; `shutdown()` breaks it (the contract fixture's step does this;
   the thread idles, harmless in tests).
7. Dependencies: `rustix` (already pinned) added to `lunix-space`; `Cargo.lock` in a separate `deps:` commit. The
   `thiserror` dependency of the skeleton is unused, as in M6.
8. `probe.rs` ships `ManualFs` and `SimFs` in the library (tests of other crates, e.g. M11, can build a gate on them).

## Spec gaps

- DESIGN 4.3 does not say whether `cache_max` includes the reserve; chosen as in 4 above.
- The iface does not say what a granted waiter does after `fatal_io`; parked requests are expired (`TimedOut`), so the
  caller sees `NoSpace` rather than `ReadOnly`. A `Waiter` state for read-only would be a small iface addition.
