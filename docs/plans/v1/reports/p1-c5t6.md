## Deviations and remarks

1. **Pin on a plain volume.** The CLI has no snapshot command and a pin must be a sealed object; an integrity volume needs its HMAC key. The cell therefore uses a **plain-mode
   volume** (form 0 envelope whose MAC is BLAKE3 over `meta_aad || envelope`, written by `tests/perf/c5t6-pin.py` from the layouts in `lunix-format`, nothing
   from the product changed) and names the commit `last_commit_seq` of the status after the fill. The engine read it as a pin (`pin_blocked` > 0). Plain mode skips the integrity
   HMAC, so its IOPS are not comparable with the integrity cells.
2. **"Attach + 30 s" and "by 43 s".** The plan does not say from where: `proposal_condemned_bytes` "before attach + 30 s" is the last 2 s sample at or before 30 s after the first
   pass line of the log (the engine's own start clock `born`); "by 43 s" and all other times of gate 3 are from fio's `job_start` of rep 1, which is 11.4-12.8 s after the attach.
   The engine's `first_proposal_ms` / `first_proposal_delete_ms` are on its own clock and are shifted by that start.
3. **Reading of fuzzy clauses.** "Stops at most 1 in 4 of 5 reps": at most one repetition with an engine stop among reps 2-5 of a block (strict), and B not above A in total.
   "IOPS within 3 %": B not below A by more than 3 %. "Outside a stop": see gate 2. Gate 7's fit is not identified (above), so the gate is judged on the equal-size cost per
   journal byte (the plan's own words), with the fit printed beside it.
4. **Block order** (arm A / B / off, one `off` block per chunk size as the plan says): 64K w4k A B B A off B A A B (b01-b09, the `off` block fifth), 16K A B off B A (b10-b14),
   zipf A B B A (b15-b18, no `off`), then the restart (b19) and pin (b20) blocks, both B. So 64K and 16K have rep 1 n = 4 and 2 per arm and one off block.
5. **Analysis fixes** (c5t6.py, the listed defects): window from fio's `job_start`; every pass clipped to the window and urgent passes (empty `phases=`) counted by wall time;
   cut cost from the exact counters, not sampled; onset from the collector's `gc: the engine's pressure level changed` line (uncoalesced); medians from the per-event lines
   (`refs: an offer was taken` for the condemn lag, `gc: a proposed segment was deleted` for the delete lag). The `Recheck` pass-line lag (the old gate) is also given.
6. **Samples.** Status and the collector status were sampled every 2 s; the 1 s status probe ran as in refsoak (`probe.tsv`). The two JSONs are kept before and after every repetition.
7. **Kill loops**: five of the ten loops, run on this build after the blocks (not concurrent with them); a first launch failed at once (the test directory was on tmpfs: `LUNIX_SPIKE_DIR` must be a real file system) and was relaunched with a real one. Not rerun: encrypted 16K and 64K, integrity 16K, recheck plain 64K and recheck integrity 16K.
8. **Smoke blocks** (two short ones, 20-40 s reps, pin and restart) preceded the run to check the harness; they are not part of the results and their output was removed.
9. `LUNIX_M=condemncut` was used for the ssh wrapper as told, with trees under `lunix-p1/c5t6`; nothing else in the host's home was touched. The result files are scrubbed
   of the remote home and user name (paths replaced by `<spike-dir>`, `<tree>`, `<home>`, `<user>`); no host address or instance detail is anywhere.
10. No `[c5t6]` entry in `tools/ownership.toml` (the id `condemncut` was used for the ssh wrapper as told); `tools/check-ownership.sh` does not know the new paths.
11. The 5-minute load wait took about 25 minutes after each block (blocked daemon threads and fio count in the load average); `tables.md` lists the load at every block start.

## Findings

* No product bug found. Nothing un-condemned, named, rejected early, drifting or failing in 20 blocks.
* Active C5 buys the cold start what the plan expected in bytes (8.5 GiB freed by 43 s against 0 in A, first delete 29.5 s against 49.2 s, IOPS +25 %, p99.9 -38 %) but
  **the 30 s grace floor bounds the first delete**, so the rep 1 stop (13 s) and throttle time (33 s) stay above their gates; only arm C (not run) can bring them down.
* The steady state is neutral: no regression in stops, longest wait, IOPS or p99.9 at 64K, 16K or zipf; proposals cost 0.06 % of the wall.
* The condemn share in steady state is a third, not 45 %: in reps 2-5 the mark still condemns most bytes. Either the 45 % target applies to rep 1 only, or active C5 does less in steady state than planned.
* The 16K mark share (16.3 %) is above the 12 % limit by the workload (14.1 % with the feature off) plus 2.9 points of B.
* The orphan rule and the Hold stage were never entered, even with a pin and a restart: they remain exercised only by the tests.
