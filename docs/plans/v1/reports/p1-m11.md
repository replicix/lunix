## What was built (`crates/lunix-engine`)

`Engine` implements `lunix_iface::block::BlockEngine` over the `lunix-iface` traits only (dependencies: format,
iface, thiserror, tracing; testkit and proptest as dev-dependencies). A `crashpoints` feature forwards to
`lunix-iface/crashpoints` (no normal edge enables it).

| module | content |
|---|---|
| `queue.rs` | `QueueHandler` per queue. Write: `SpaceGate::reserve(JournalReserved, record_bytes)` (park on a `Waiter`, `ENOSPC` on refusal), then under the queue's in-flight lock `try_reserve` (assigns `write_seq`) **and** registration of the number as in flight, `EngineHooks::sequenced`, `append`. On the `Append` completion: overlay insert, then the number leaves the in-flight set, then completion (FUA first joins the next flush group). `Full`/`NoSpace` from the lane give the space back and park the tag FIFO (no number consumed); a cut's C9 or a lane completion wakes it. Writes over 1 MiB are split into 256-block records. Read: overlay `lookup`; journal pieces via `submit_read` (a reclaimed location falls back to the map); holes are zeros; the rest per chunk: `try_get_cached` hole/hit/miss, decoded-chunk cache on the queue thread, everything else to the read helper pool. DISCARD/WRITE_ZEROES: one hole record, overlay hole. EINVAL for unaligned, empty, past-the-end or over-`max_io_buf_bytes` requests; EIO for writes once read-only, poisoned or failed. |
| `flush.rs` | Flush groups: waiters gather, `upto = Journal::last_issued()` read after the waiters are taken, one `submit_group_flush` per group, at most one in flight, the next accumulating; the callback only posts results and wakes queues. A refused submission completes its waiters with EIO. |
| `coord.rs` | C1-C10 (below), triggers (`T` counted from the first tick that saw the volume dirty, `B` = overlay dirty bytes, journal fill > 0.5, a write parked on a full ring, pending `moved`), forced commits, grow, quiesce, the C10 GC hook. |
| `recover.rs` | `Engine::open` in the order below, `RecoveryReport`. |
| `read.rs` | The R1 reader (`ExtentReader`), the stale-hint fallback, the read helper pool. |
| `destage.rs` | The `Destager` seam to M9 and `SimpleDestager`, the in-crate stand-in (whole-chunk assembly with an R1 base read for partial chunks, zero chunks and all-hole chunks as map holes, dedup against the cut and the index with D17 confirmation of `Hinted` hits, raw codec, every segment sealed before one map transaction commits, segments of a failed cut deleted, index inserts returned). |
| `cache.rs` | The decoded-chunk cache: CLOCK, bounded by `read_cache_max` bytes, keyed by `(hint, id prefix)`. |
| `stats.rs`, `points.rs`, `config.rs`, `deps.rs`, `error.rs` | Counters (snapshot as `EngineStats`, persisted in the checkpoint and restored), crash points, settings, wiring, typed mount errors (`Rollback`, `Fork`, `TipUnauthenticated`, `BrokenChain`, `JournalDamage`, ...). |

**C1-C10 as implemented.** C1 `c = last_issued()` (clamped below a poisoned number), wait until no queue has an
in-flight number `<= c`; C2 `overlay.cut(c)`, headroom bounds (`reserve_headroom(Segments)`, `reserve_headroom(Headroom)`);
(`c` first bounded so the cut dirties at most `max_leaves_per_cut` leaves); C3 pending `moved` records as
`rehint`s in their own transaction (deferred on failure), then destage (which seals every segment it wrote
before its map transaction commits); C4 `new_segments` fixed, `transfer(Segments)` to their real size; C5 grow
the map if asked, `map.seal` into a pack, seal it (an empty pack is dropped),
`transfer(Headroom, Map)`; C6 the FULL (`commit_seq = parent + 1`, epoch 0, `parent_hash`, `created_at = max(now,
parent.created_at)`, `cut_seq = c`, `chain_floor = max(parent.chain_floor, seq - (retain_last - 1))`, `index_packs =
index.runs()`, compaction output segments of the applied `moved` records added to `new_segments`), `seal_commit`,
`put`; C7 checkpoint (lane tails after `c`, `id_high`, stats), then the rollback floor `{commit_seq, hash}`; C8 index
inserts for the new extents, `persist(upto)` with `upto` the highest segment a durable commit names, `Index` trued up
to the bytes of every run; C9 `reclaim(tail_after(c))`, `release(JournalReserved, min(reclaimed, held))`,
`overlay.release`, leftover headroom released, queues woken; C10 one `Collector::step` when the map root equals the
tip's and no leaf is dirty. A failure inside C3..C6 that is not a crash marks the engine failed (no more commits,
writes EIO; reopen recovers); C7-C9 failures are logged (the hint, the floor and the index are recoverable; the
journal is reclaimed at the next cut). Destage failures are classified (see Review fixes).

**Startup (`Engine::open`).** Read the tip, authenticate it (a tip that does not is `TipUnauthenticated`; no
fallback), require a FULL, `verify_chain` down to its `chain_floor`; R4 against the floor (`Rollback`, `Fork`)
before anything changes; open the retained commits and compute the named segments, packs and the high-water mark;
the orphan sweep; `ids.observe(max(mark, sweep.highest_id_before, checkpoint.id_high))`; `index.catch_up` over the
segments the chain names (after the sweep); the map at the tip's root; the checkpoint hint only if its
`last_commit_seq <= tip`; `journal.recover(hint, tip.cut_seq)`; damage and records beyond a gap are logged here,
before any lane opens, and `on_journal_damage = refuse` returns `JournalDamage`; the overlay rebuilt from the
records; the floor raised to the tip when absent or lower (`first_attach` reported and logged); the ledger charged
with the bytes of every segment, pack, commit, index run and the recovered records. Lanes are opened by
`queue_handler`, so the journal's stale-tail wipe happens after the log line. Recovery writes only the sweep and the
floor, so a crash during it is another recovery (the model test crashes at every `engine.recover.*` point).

Crash points (`CRASH_POINTS`): `engine.c1.waited`, `c2.cut`, `c3.destaged`, `c4.named`, `c5.map_sealed`,
`c6.committed`, `c7.checkpoint`, `c7.floor`, `c8.index`, `c9.reclaimed`, `engine.recover.{verified,swept,caught_up,journal,floor}`.
Each is a `lunix_iface::crashpoint!` (SIGKILL in the `crashpoints` build) plus `EngineHooks::crash_at`, which
stops the engine in-process for the fakes.

## Decisions taken where the plan was silent

1. **C1 visibility.** A queue takes its in-flight lock around `try_reserve` and the registration of the number,
   and drops the number only after the overlay insert. The coordinator reads `last_issued()` first, then each
   queue's set, so a number `<= c` issued before `c` was read is always seen. This is what makes every commit a
   cut with the frozen `Journal` API (which has no global "inserted" watermark).
2. **Index inserts after C6, not after C4.** The extents of a cut enter the index only when the commit naming
   their segments is durable, so no failed cut can leave dedup pointing at an orphan in-process (N2); C8 persists them.
3. **No map entry names an unsealed segment**: the destager seals every segment before its map transaction
   commits (lead note on M9's review), so C4 in this crate is the ledger true-up of segments already durable.
   (An earlier version kept the last segment open until C4 and made readers wait for it; replaced.)
4. **Compaction output is named by the cut that applies its re-hints**: the `new.seg` of every applied `moved`
   record joins `new_segments` (M17 rule 4); otherwise the open-time sweep would delete a segment the map
   references. `Moved` has no chunk number, so C3 finds the chunks by walking the tip's leaves for the old hints.
5. **`T` counts from the first tick that saw the volume dirty**, not from the last commit, so the first write after
   an idle hour does not commit at once; the idle volume cuts nothing.
6. **Bounded cuts** (changed by the review to leaves). `max_leaves_per_cut` (default `map_dirty_leaves_max / 2`,
   with `map_fanout` from the descriptor) lowers `c` before the cut: `cut(c')` holds the chunks with a version
   `<= c'`, so taking chunks in the order of their oldest visible write, `c'` stops just before the first chunk
   whose leaf would exceed the cap. DISCARD and WRITE_ZEROES records are split to half the cap's leaves, so a
   cut can stop between records. A `MapFull` that still happens (a superseded version can add a chunk) retries a smaller cut
   (`c' = prev + (c - prev) / 2`); any `c'` in `(prev, c]` is a cut, nothing is split.
7. **`JournalReserved` releases are clamped to what the engine holds**: after a restart the engine charges only the
   recovered records (> floor) while `reclaim` reports every record of a recycled file (M3 decision 6).
8. **The read cache is keyed by `(hint, prefix)`**, not by chunk id (the queue thread only has the prefix);
   locations are never reused, so entries cannot go stale.
9. **A FUA write joins the next flush group** after its append completes; the group's `upto >=` its number, so it
   is covered with everything below it (ADR-28's cut at its own seq).
10. **The R1 fallback**: an `Auth` failure at the hint counts an `extent_auth_failures` event and still tries the
    index candidates (other locations only, each opened against its full id); `NotFound`/`Corrupt` at the hint is a
    stale hint and counts nothing. Swapped, older or damaged extents at the hint therefore fail with EIO.
11. **Grow** requires a multiple of the chunk size; it is a forced commit with the new size, the size becomes
    visible after C6, then the resize hook runs.

## Requests to the lead

1. **`state/pins` v2 floor in `VolumeDir`** (blocks M12 wiring of R4, not M11). `VolumeDir::local_pins` /
   `store_local_pins` carry `LocalPins`, which drops the rollback floor (format amendment report, deviation 1).
   The engine takes the floor through its own `RollbackFloorStore` trait meanwhile. Wanted additive diff in
   `lunix-iface/src/store.rs`:
   ```rust
   /// The rollback floor of `state/pins` v2 (D19); `None` when absent.
   fn rollback_floor(&self) -> Result<Option<lunix_format::local::RollbackFloor>> { Ok(None) }
   /// Raises the floor atomically (tmp, fsync, rename, dir fsync), keeping the rest of `state/pins`.
   /// Refused (`Refused`) for a lower `max_commit_seq`.
   fn raise_rollback_floor(&self, floor: &lunix_format::local::RollbackFloor) -> Result<()> {
       let _ = floor; Err(Error::Unsupported { what: "rollback floor" })
   }
   ```
   with `lunix-store` implementing both over `LocalPinsFile`; M12 then adapts `VolumeDir` to `RollbackFloorStore`.
2. **`Moved` should carry the chunk number** (`ci: ChunkNo`): the engine now walks the tip's leaves to find the
   chunks of moved extents (decision 4), a full walk per cut that applies moves.
3. **Destage seam (M9 alignment, lead note of 2026-11-12).** The engine defines `Destager`/`DestageCtx`/
   `DestageOutcome`; M12 adapts M9's `destage_cut(&OverlayCut, &DestageDeps)` to it. Checked against
   `p1/m9-destage` (2f37325 and the lead's two notes on its revision) and adapted: the cut is borrowed (the
   engine keeps it for `Overlay::release`); `DestageOutcome::segments` lists every segment, **all sealed
   before the map transaction commits** (no `open`/`seal_open()` any more; this crate never depended on them
   after the second note); index entries are returned and inserted only after C6 is durable (M9's
   `insert_into_index()`, never on a failed commit); on an error nothing is applied to the map and the
   destager deletes its segments; `DestageCtx` carries the `SpaceGate` for `ReadOnly` and `fatal_io` only, the
   C2/C4 ledger work stays in the coordinator. The adapter maps `DestagedSegment.entries` to
   `index_inserts`, `DestagedSegment.sealed` to `segments` and `DestageStats` to the counters. Error mapping:
   M9's "cut too large for the map" (permanent) should come through as `MapFull` so the coordinator cuts
   smaller; with `max_leaves_per_cut` it should not occur. Retryable errors fail this commit attempt (the next
   trigger retries); other errors after C3 changed the map stop commits in the process (reopen recovers).
4. `GcControl` is not implemented here (the iface says "engine wiring"); M12 can post requests to the
   coordinator mutex through `Engine::commit_now` and a new `Engine::run_gc` when M17 lands.

## Spec gaps found

- DESIGN §4.4 lists "orphan sweep, then catch-up" after the journal; the plan's step 6 says "orphan sweep first".
  Order used: verify, R4, sweep, catch-up, map, checkpoint, journal, overlay, floor (sweep before catch-up as both
  require; R4 before any change).
- The plan says "verify the last link" in step 6 and "the chain is verified from the tip down to `chain_floor`" at
  its end; the engine verifies down to `chain_floor` (cheap with `last:2`).
- C4 "seal the open segment(s)" after C3 "map updated in memory" lets a reader of a dirty chunk's unwritten blocks
  reach a map entry naming an unsealed (`*.open`, unreadable) segment; DESIGN §8.2 step 3 should say the
  segments are sealed before the map transaction commits (decision 3).
- Truncate-tip loses journal records of the set-aside commit that C9 already reclaimed (offline repair, expected;
  worth a sentence in the fsck docs).

## Deviations and open choices

- **Read path:** every store read goes to the helper pool, including small decompressions (the plan allows ≤ 16 KiB
  on the queue thread); cache hits are served inline. Each read allocates its assembly buffer (M16 measures).
- **C4 is a ledger step**: sealing moved into C3 (the destager); its crash point is now `engine.c4.named` (the
  point where `new_segments` is fixed).
- **`SimpleDestager`** stores raw only and is single-threaded; compression policy, worker pool and stage timings
  beyond hash/dedup/seal/append are M9's.
- **R1 over the encrypted codec** and the lease part of R4 (`open_lease_fresh`) are not testable here: the testkit
  has no encrypted codec and a local volume has no lease. M12 runs the R1 cases over `lunix-crypto`.
- The ledger is checked exactly after every commit (outside the C2-C9 window); inside the window it is not
  sampled.
- The default model size in `cargo test` is 100 x 2000 per chunk size (about two minutes); the gate run uses
  `LUNIX_MODEL_CASES=500`.
