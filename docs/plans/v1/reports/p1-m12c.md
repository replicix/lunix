**Every collector call runs under the coordinator mutex** on the calling thread (a call thread, the gate's
thread, the coordinator itself), through one helper (`with_collector`). It builds the view like the background
step (without cloning the retained commits), refuses `Busy` when the map is not at the tip, and publishes the
collector's status after the call. Every entry checks `closing` and the failed flag under that mutex.

**`run_pass(false)`** asks for a pass now (`request_pass`; `Unsupported` if the collector does not take
requests) and steps it to its end. Between two steps it releases the mutex and waits on a condvar: 5 ms after a
step that worked, 100 ms in the grace or after a compaction step that read nothing (rate cap). So cuts, a quiesce
and the ladder get the mutex between steps, and no core spins. While it waits, the published status counts it
(`requests`).

It returns once a pass that started after the call ended: when the collector was idle, the next pass to end (the
requested one, or an urgent pass); when a pass was in progress (its view predates the call), the one after it.
**The answer covers every pass that ended while the call waited** (re-review S-1): counts summed, `bytes_freed`
and `segments_compacted` from the collector's counters, `pass` the newest evidence number. A call that finds a
pass in its grace therefore reports what that pass deleted too, not only the (maybe empty) pass after it.

A pass the collector drops is asked for again, four asks in all, then `Refused`. A map ahead of the tip is retried
for 50 steps, then `Busy`. **`run_pass(true)`** is one `Collector::dry_run`. **`run_urgent`** is one
`Collector::urgent`; its moves go to the next cut.

**Quiesce** sets `closing`, notifies the condvar, then takes the coordinator mutex for the final commit: no
collector call starts after it (requested, urgent, or the background step, which now checks `closing` too), and a
waiting requested pass ends `Closed` at once. `closing` is one-way, also when
the final commit failed (documented on the field and on `Engine::quiesce`).

**Out of space.**

- Compaction re-hints are applied right after the destage succeeded (still before C5), so a cut whose destage
  fails leaves the map at the tip (review B-1). They share the map's dirty-leaf cap with the cut's destage: they
  go in leaf order, at most `max_leaves_per_cut` leaves, halved on `MapFull` down to one leaf; the moves of the
  leaves left over stay for a later cut, and while moves wait for room they trigger a cut on their own only once
  per commit interval (each such cut walks the tip's leaves) (re-review M-1).
- A commit that fails with `NoSpace` at any step (the destage, the retry of an unpersisted index run) runs the
  urgent pass on the coordinator, wanting the dirty bytes. It runs at least 2 s after the end of the last urgent
  pass of either kind (the ladder's counts only when a pass ran). The pause doubles, up to 60 s, while those passes
  free nothing; it is at most the floor grace after a pass that condemned something, and back to 2 s after a pass
  that freed bytes or a successful commit (re-review M-5).
- A tick whose commit failed still steps the collector when the map is at the tip.

**The ladder.** `open_volume` registers `CompactStep { engine: Weak<Engine>, runs, borrows }`. It runs on the
gate's thread, takes a borrow, then upgrades the engine and calls `run_urgent(want)`; with the gate closed, or on a
quiesced or dropped engine, it frees 0. `runs` counts the runs that did not fail (a failed one is in the gate's
`step_errors`). No deadlock: the coordinator never waits
for the gate (it only uses `reserve_headroom`, `release`, `transfer`), and a write parks in the gate before it
takes a `write_seq`, so a cut never waits for a parked write.

**Daemon.**

- `Gc` holds the volume mutex only to clone the engine and the borrow gate, then takes a borrow (none once a
  detach closed the gate: `Busy`). One non-dry `Gc` per volume at a time; a second answers `Busy` at once, so a
  client that gave up and retries does not stack call threads. Not attached: `Invalid`. `Closed`: `Busy`
  ("detached or quiesced while the pass ran").
- `GcStatus` reads a `GcStatusHandle` (the engine's published status) kept per attached volume, set at attach and
  removed at detach. It holds no engine. Not attached: idle.
- Detach drops the device, closes the borrow gate and waits for the borrows (`Gc`, the compact step) to end, then
  drops the stack: the engine's threads are stopped and `LOCK` is free when the detach answers. **Exception:** a
  borrow still running after 30 s (a collector unit stuck in I/O) keeps the engine; the detach then answers with a
  warning in the log and `LOCK` is released only when that borrow ends (stated in `gc.md` and on
  `Daemon::release`). The gate replaces the `strong_count` poll and its race with `Weak::upgrade` (re-review M-3).
- A non-dry `Gc` is exempt from `call_timeout` (it ends on detach or quiesce; the client's `--timeout` still
  applies). Its connection thread checks the client every second (`POLLRDHUP`) and stops waiting when it hung up;
  the call thread finishes the pass (one per volume, bounded, ended by a detach or quiesce) (re-review M-4).

## Deviations

- **`lunix-iface` changed** (two default methods on `Collector`): `GcControl::status` must not wait for the
  coordinator, and the engine names no implementation crate; a pass on request needs a way past the pass interval.
- **`Gc` does not hold the volume mutex for the pass** (the daemon's rule is one mutex per volume per call): a pass
  lasts at least the grace, and detach or `QuiesceAll` must not wait for it. Quiesce ends the call; detach waits for
  it to let go of the engine (one step at most).
- **The engine runs the urgent pass itself when a commit fails for space**, besides the gate's ladder, and applies
  re-hints after the destage: both are in the M11 coordinator (`coord.rs`).
- **`server.rs`**: a non-dry `Gc` waits without the `call_timeout` and stops waiting when the client hangs up.
- **`lunix-iface`**: `GcStatusSnapshot::requests` (a protocol field; WIP, no compatibility kept) and the
  `BlockEngine::quiesce` doc.
- **`tools/ci.sh`**: `stack_gc_ladder` and `control_gc` join the release stack tests.
